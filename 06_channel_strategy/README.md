# 06 Channel partner expansion strategy (D6) + Inject 3

Strata Sense Technologies, its partners and competitors are fictional; all data are synthetic, built for an MBA capstone.

- `D6_Channel_Strategy_Deck.pptx`: 15 slides. Role of channel, partner types and mix, keep/fix/exit for 14 signed partners, scored shortlist of 12 prospects, programme design (tiers, margins, deal registration, conflict rules, certification), channel P&L, gross-margin floor test, break-even productivity and its likelihood, Inject 3 response, team, scorecard and ask.
- `D6_Partner_Economics.xlsx`: formula-driven model (blue inputs, yellow key assumptions). Partner ramp, channel P&L (substitution and incremental lenses), GM floor, break-even, Inject 3, partner decisions, prospect scoring, Monte Carlo results, checks.
- `D6_90Day_Launch_Plan.docx`: two-page launch plan with owners, measures, budget, risks and partner-by-partner day-90 targets.
- `risk_sim*.json`: output of the Monte Carlo scripts (plan case and downside).
- Data room addition (in /data): DR6 partner file (14 signed partners, 12 prospects).

Key results: break-even ≈ ₹0.24 cr sourced ARR per active partner per year (substitution basis); FY29 partner share 25.3% in plan but only ~46% likely; FY27 channel costs ₹0.9 cr more than direct; gross margin after partner margin stays ≥ 67.6%.

AI use: drafting and analysis produced with an AI assistant (Claude); DR6 is synthetic case data.
